GTA 6 beginner tips

What specialist guides repeat most so you don't waste money or time in your first hours. No story spoilers.

Quick answer

Don't carry much cash, save manually before big missions, change clothes and car to lose the police, and work out at the gym for more health and stamina.

The 10 tips

  1. Don't carry much cash

    If you die you can lose a big chunk of the cash you carry. Keep it in your safehouse stash or in the bank as soon as it unlocks.

  2. Save manually before big missions

    According to guides, there's no way to replay finished missions. A manual save before each big mission lets you go back if something goes wrong.

  3. Change clothes and car to escape

    Police track your clothing and vehicle. Changing outfit and ditching the car helps a lot to lose your heat level.

  4. Watch which cars you steal

    Some cars carry trackers. If you want to sell one or stay unnoticed, pick a car without one.

  5. Keep an eye on fuel and engine

    Vehicles have fuel and engine health. Refuel and repair before a long trip or a getaway.

  6. Work out at the gym

    Strength raises your max health and cardio your stamina. It's worth some time early on.

  7. Use Focus mode

    It helps you spot enemies through walls and hidden clues, like safe combinations.

  8. Try stealth

    Many missions and robberies can be done quietly. It usually costs less than ending up in a shootout with the police.

  9. Explore Leonida region by region

    The map is huge. Learning it one region at a time helps you get your bearings and not miss activities.

  10. Save cheats for later

    Many cheats unlock after the story or through challenges, and several guides say they disable trophies and saving while active.

FAQ

What should I do first in GTA 6?

Save manually after the prologue, keep your money somewhere safe as soon as you can and visit the gym to boost health and stamina.

How do I lose the police in GTA 6?

Break line of sight and change your vehicle and clothes: police track both.

Can you replay missions in GTA 6?

According to published guides, no. That's why it's worth saving manually before each big mission.
