Every briefing

One briefing every morning. Every story links to its source.

  1. Saturday, November 28, 2026

    Alinea estimates 53 million GTA VI copies sold and $4.7 billion (estimate)

    According to Forbes, Alinea Analytics estimates 53 million copies sold and $4.7 billion eight days after launch. Also, a traffic clip revives an old theory about fast drivers.

  2. Friday, November 27, 2026

    GTA 6 on PC by unofficial routes: illegal and risky for malware, warns Windows Central

    Windows Central warns that attempts to run GTA 6 on PC rely on stolen files, are illegal and can bring malware. Also, the South Park finale puts the game in an episode.

  3. Thursday, November 26, 2026

    The 2022 leak's author sentenced to seven years and free the same day, per TweakTown

    TweakTown reports the person behind the 2022 leak was sentenced to seven years and released on licence that same day. Also, a ten-year-old song is back on the charts thanks to GTA VI.

  4. Wednesday, November 25, 2026

    Two new GTA VI secrets: an "It"-style clown and the Belville house messages

    A nod to the clown from "It" in a Vice Beach hotel that seems to depend on the time of day, and an NPC's messages in the Belville house that appeared in San Andreas in 2021.

  5. Tuesday, November 24, 2026

    GTA VI's first patch doesn't fix the PS5 crash with error CE-108255-1

    According to Notebookcheck, update 01.002.002 doesn't resolve PS5 error CE-108255-1. Plus, a secret portal leads to 2002's Vice City and Tommy Vercetti's shirt.

  6. Monday, November 23, 2026

    GTA VI gets its first patch with notes: fixes crashes, autosave and phone calls

    Rockstar releases its first post-launch patch, with official notes and a staged rollout on PS5. Cyberleek says it has dropped the missing file of its Xbox build.

  7. Sunday, November 22, 2026

    Rockstar puts GTA VI copies sold before launch at 32.5 million

    Rockstar reportedly gave the missing copies figure: 32.5 million in preorders alone, alongside the $3.79 billion in revenue. Plus nearly 8 million concurrent livestream viewers and the PS5 update.

  8. Saturday, November 21, 2026

    Take-Two: GTA VI made $3.79 billion between preorders and day one

    An official Take-Two figure, review bombing in user scores while the press holds a 99, and a bug that closes the game on PS5. No official copies-sold number.

  9. Friday, November 20, 2026

    Rockstar announces GTA VI is available; Sensor Tower estimates 19 million copies

    An estimated 19 million copies on day one and sign-in problems on PSN. A group claims to have released an unauthenticated Xbox build, and a house hidden in 2021 turns up in the map.

  10. Thursday, November 19, 2026

    GTA 6 is on sale: it unlocks at local midnight in each time zone

    After 13 years of waiting, GTA VI launches today on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S with record press scores. Preload demand spiked internet traffic in some regions.

  11. Wednesday, November 18, 2026

    GTA 6 now has a score: 99 on Metacritic and 98 on OpenCritic hours before launch

    First reviews are excellent: 99 on Metacritic and 98 on OpenCritic. A fake letter about a delay to 2027 is also debunked and the 2022 leak's author will be sentenced on the 23rd.

  12. Tuesday, November 17, 2026

    Nearly 90% of preorders reportedly for the Ultimate edition, per a report (estimate)

    Two third-party estimates on GTA VI's business two days before launch: the Ultimate edition's share of preorders and Morgan Stanley's forecast of 40 million copies.

  13. Monday, November 16, 2026

    GTA VI reportedly targets 30 fps on consoles, PS5 Pro included, three days before launch

    Several outlets agree the game will run at 30 fps even on PS5 Pro, and the preload open since November 10 came with a teaser featuring a Tove Lo track.

  14. Sunday, November 15, 2026

    GTA VI will be playable on Xbox cloud from 7 a.m. Spain time on the 19th

    Xbox cloud play with limits, warnings about boxed-edition and PS5 codes, and three unconfirmed rumors about multiplayer, budget and review copies.

  15. Saturday, November 14, 2026

    GTA VI gets its first mandatory patch five days before launch

    Mandatory patch 1.002.000, a 24-hour YouTube marathon, lighter versions in Japan and Australia, codes visible through some boxes and the GTA VI effect on the economy.
