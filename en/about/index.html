About

Leónida Hoy

The 5 GTA VI stories that matter today, each with a linked source and explained in plain words. Every morning.

What we do

Every morning we read the GTA VI news and keep the five stories that really matter. One line per story, what changed and a link to the original source. No filler.

Our standards

  • What's new comes first. Each briefing brings what we hadn't covered yet, most recent first; if something was announced earlier, we say so.
  • Rumors kept separate and labeled. Only the most talked-about ones, with who says it and how reliable it is. If nobody identifiable publishes it, it doesn't appear here.
  • A link under every story. You can always read the original.
  • Estimates are labeled as estimates. Third-party figures are never presented as official.

Two reading levels

Use the "I follow every update / I'm new here" switch: fans get the technical detail and newcomers get the plain-words explanation.

In two languages

Every briefing is published in English and Spanish. Switch language with the EN / ES selector in the header.

Contact and corrections

Spotted a mistake or have a sourced tip? Open an issue in the project repository. We fix things quickly and say so.
