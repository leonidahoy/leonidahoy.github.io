Rockstar's head of publishing: they would have kept working on the game

Tuesday, November 17, 2026

According to EGW, relaying a Guardian interview, Rockstar's head of publishing said the team would have kept polishing GTA VI if the decision had been solely theirs.

In plain words

Rockstar admits it could have kept tweaking the game, but launch is here.

The details

The results don't give the interview date, but the statement is still current two days before launch. We include no direct quotes and there's no detail on what would have been changed.

Why it matters

It helps explain why the game was delayed twice before release.
