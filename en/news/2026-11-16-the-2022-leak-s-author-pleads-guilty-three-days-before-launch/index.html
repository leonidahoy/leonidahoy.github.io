The 2022 leak's author pleads guilty three days before launch

Monday, November 16, 2026

The person behind the 2022 leak, which spread about 60 minutes of in-development footage, pleaded guilty on November 16. Forbes mentions extortion of Rockstar and GamesRadar puts the cost at about $5 million, according to reports.

In plain words

The person who stole and published game material in 2022 has admitted the charges. Sentencing will come later.

The details

Outlets place the case in a London court, where the person also answered for other hacks. The $5 million figure is a press estimate, not an official Rockstar number. No sentencing date has been published yet.

Why it matters

It closes, at least legally, one of the most serious episodes in the game's history.
