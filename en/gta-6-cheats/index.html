Frequently asked questions

How do you enter cheats in GTA 6?

Take out your phone by pressing up on the D-pad, hold L1 and R1 (LB and RB on Xbox) and, while holding them, press the cheat's sequence on the D-pad.

Why isn't a cheat working?

Most unlock after completing a challenge or finishing the story. Check each cheat's requirement and keep holding L1 and R1 while you press the sequence.

Is there a money cheat in GTA 6?

Yes: according to several guides it gives $250,000 and unlocks after you finish the main story.

Can you use cheats in GTA Online?

No. Cheats are for story mode. Using cheats, mods or glitches online breaks Rockstar's rules.
