Take-Two: GTA VI made $3.79 billion between preorders and day one

What changed: According to Variety, Take-Two says GTA VI preorders and first-day sales added up to $3.79 billion, and calls it "the biggest entertainment launch in history." A spokesperson wouldn't say how many copies were sold.

In short: It's the first official sales figure. It comes from the company itself, not an analyst.

Read more →via Variety
02
Impact

Metacritic: the press holds a 99, but users sink the score

What changed: Insider Gaming reports GTA VI is still at 99 out of 100 after 152 press reviews, but that about half of user scores are 49 or below, pointing to possible review bombing.

In short: Press scores are near-perfect and user scores are all over the place. Trust outlet reviews more than the user average.

Read more →via Insider Gaming
03
Heads-up

A bug is closing GTA VI on PS5 and sending some players back to the menu

What changed: According to DualShockers, a bug is closing the game on PS5 mid-session. Neither Sony nor Rockstar has given a cause or a fix, and reports point only to PS5.

In short: If the game closes on your PS5, you're not alone. Save manually and make sure your console is up to date.

Read more →via DualShockers, DLCompare

A copies number is going around, but the company hasn't confirmed it. Treat it as provisional.
