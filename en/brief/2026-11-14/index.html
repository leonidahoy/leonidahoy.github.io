The 5 that matter today

How do you want to read it?

01
UpdateBiggest story

Patch 1.002.000 is here and it's mandatory

What changed: Rockstar released the first update on November 13: about 1.07 GB on PS5 and 2.18 GB on Xbox Series. It's required to play on the 19th and has no patch notes. It rolled out in stages and reached everyone by 23:00 UTC.

In short: You'll need to download a small update before you can play. If you already preloaded the game, switch your console on and let it download.

Read more →via tbreak, ComicBook.com
02
Event

YouTube will stream 24 straight hours of GTA VI on launch day

What changed: It starts November 19 at 12:00 a.m. ET (6:00 a.m. in Spain), with an RDCWorld pre-show the night before. More than 30 creators will play live and it wraps up from Miami.

In short: If you don't have the game yet, you can watch it live for free on YouTube all launch day.

Read more →via KeenGamer, Notebookcheck
03
Controversy

The preload is smaller in Japan and Australia

What changed: According to Kotaku, the Japanese version is about 1.2 GB smaller and the Australia and New Zealand version is missing a file of roughly 704 KB. People fear censorship, but Rockstar hasn't confirmed any cuts.

In short: In some countries the game takes up a bit less space. Nobody knows yet if content is missing; so far it's just suspicion.

Read more →via Kotaku
04
Heads-up

Some physical boxes reveal the code with a flashlight

What changed: VideoCardz reports that on certain physical editions (which are a code in a box) the download code can be read without opening it. Tests on US and UK copies failed; some European ones may be less protected.

In short: If you buy the boxed edition, get it from a store you trust and check the code works as soon as you open it.

Read more →via VideoCardz
05
Impact

CNBC: GTA VI may show up in food delivery, tourism and time off

What changed: CNBC looks at how the launch on the 19th could boost food delivery orders, vacation days taken and even tourism in Miami.

In short: The game is so big that even economists expect lots of people to stay home that week.

Read more →via CNBC

More news

Studio

Rockstar's UK staff vote on recognizing a union

Around 1,600 workers vote from November 12 to 26 on recognizing the IWGB union, right across launch week. A yes would make it the biggest recognized union in the UK games industry.

While the game launches, the people who made it decide whether to join a union.

Read more →via PC Gamer, Insider Gaming
Gameplay

Rob Nelson: for the first time, a GTA encourages empathy

Rockstar North's co-studio head says GTA VI includes mechanics that let you play "the good guy" if you want, something new for the series.

You'll be able to choose to do the right thing in many situations, not just cause chaos.

Read more →via Insider Gaming

Reliable rumors

The most talked-about things that aren't confirmed yet. Only with a named source and a reliability rating — take them with a pinch of salt.

RumorHigh reliability

Reviews reportedly drop November 18, a day early

NateTheHate posted that Rockstar has sent review copies with an embargo on the 18th, and Insider Gaming says it verified this independently. Rockstar hasn't confirmed it.

You may be able to read the first review scores on Wednesday the 18th.

Who says it: NateTheHate · Insider Gaming

Read more →via Insider Gaming, Beebom
RumorMedium reliability

The PC version would arrive in Q1 2027

GamesBeat, citing four anonymous industry sources, points to January–March 2027. Take-Two hasn't confirmed it, and its revenue outlook reportedly doesn't include PC before March.

If you play on PC, you may have to wait a few months.

Who says it: GamesBeat (4 anonymous sources)

Read more →via TechSpot, MP1st
RumorLow reliability

The new GTA Online won't be there on the 19th, but may come soon

A look at the game files found references to the online mode, and a leaker talks about a debut within a month of launch. The only sure thing: it won't be available on the 19th.

Multiplayer would arrive a few weeks after the game.

Who says it: Dataminers · leaker with no verified track record

Read more →via Notebookcheck, OpenCritic
