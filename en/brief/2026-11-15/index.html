01
LaunchBiggest story

GTA VI reaches Xbox cloud at 1:00 a.m. ET on November 19

What changed: Microsoft says the game will be playable by streaming on Xbox One and Xbox Series X|S from 1:00 a.m. ET on the 19th (7:00 a.m. in Spain). It runs at Xbox Series S settings and isn't planned for PC, phones or TVs at launch.

In short: If you don't have a new console, you'll be able to play over the internet from an Xbox, as long as your market has the service.

Read more →via Windows Central, Notebookcheck
02
Heads-up

Boxed-edition buyers say their code is missing or already redeemed

What changed: According to VGTimes, some physical-edition buyers say their box has an already redeemed code or none at all. With no disc, no code means no install. Rockstar hadn't commented as of that report.

In short: If you buy the box, open it and redeem the code right away. If it fails, keep your receipt and box and contact the store and support.

Read more →via VGTimes, tbreak
03
Heads-up

PS5 codes are region-locked and Japanese ones expire

What changed: Physical PS5 codes only work on a PlayStation Network account from the same region they were sold in. According to Dexerto, Japanese codes expire 170 days after launch; Insider Gaming says Xbox isn't region-locked.

In short: Buy the game in your account's region and redeem the code early, especially if you buy in Japan.

Read more →via Insider Gaming, Dexerto

More news

Launch

Take-Two confirms GTA VI has gone gold: the launch build is locked

According to GameRant, Take-Two has confirmed GTA VI has entered the "gold" phase, meaning development of the launch version is finished four days before release.

The game that ships on the 19th is done; what remains is tweaks and later patches.

Read more →via GameRant
Heads-up

US prices: $79.99 for the standard edition and $99.99 for Ultimate

Variety notes GTA VI launches November 19 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S with the standard edition at $79.99 and the Ultimate at $99.99. Euro prices in Spain may vary by store.

Before you buy, compare the price at your store and check what each edition includes.

Read more →via Variety
Gameplay

Rockstar details a PS5 Pro upgrade: ray-traced shadows

According to GameRant, Rockstar has explained that the PS5 Pro version adds ray-traced shadows, which neither the standard PS5 nor the Xbox Series X|S offer.

If you have a PS5 Pro, you'll see slightly more realistic shadows than on other consoles.
