GTA 6 patches and problems

Updates coming to the game and the most common problems, with their status. Reliable sources only: no forum rumors.

Status now · Being tracked

No day-one patch with official notes. There were PlayStation Network sign-in problems at launch (Sony hasn't explained the cause) and a bug that closes the game on PS5 and crashes that lose progress remain open.

History

  1. November 21ProblemUnresolvedPS5

    A bug closes the game on PS5 and returns to the menu

    According to DualShockers, a bug is closing GTA VI on PS5 mid-session. Neither Sony nor Rockstar has given a cause or a fix, and reports point only to PS5. Tip: save manually and keep your console up to date.

    Source: DualShockers
  2. November 20ProblemUnresolvedPS5Xbox Series

    Crashes losing progress despite autosave

    Some PS5 and Xbox players report crashes and restarts that cost them hours of play, even with the autosave system. Rockstar hasn't published a fix or a statement. Tip: save manually when you can.

    Source: Notebookcheck
  3. November 19ProblemUnresolvedPS5

    PlayStation Network sign-in problems at launch

    Thousands of PS5 players got a "services are busy" message when signing in, and for some the game wouldn't start. In some regions the PlayStation Store also went down. Sony hasn't explained the cause and Rockstar hasn't issued a statement.

    Sources: Newsweek XDA The Hollywood Reporter Cybernews
  4. November 16UpdateNo official notesPS5Xbox Series

    Second pre-launch update (01.002.001) Version 01.002.001

    A second update arrived before anyone could play: about 602.5 MB on PS5 and around 1.59 GB on Xbox. Rockstar hasn't published notes on what it changes.

    Source: Forbes
  5. November 13UpdateNo official notesPS5Xbox Series

    First mandatory update (about 1 GB)

    Download-tracking accounts spotted a first mandatory update of around 1 GB. Rockstar didn't announce it or publish notes.

    Source: Forbes

Got a problem? What to do

  1. 1First. Check whether the problem is widespread on the PlayStation Network or Xbox status page. If it is, all you can do is wait.
  2. 2Account, download, codes or refunds. That's for PlayStation or Xbox support, depending on your console.
  3. 3Bugs in the game. Report them to Rockstar Support. Keep your console updated and leave free space for patches.
  4. 4Don't lose progress. Save manually often: autosave doesn't save all the time.

FAQ

Does GTA 6 have a day-one patch?

There were two updates before launch, but Rockstar hasn't published official notes on what they change.

Why can't I get into GTA 6 on PS5?

At launch there were PlayStation Network sign-in problems. Check PlayStation's service status: if there's a general outage, all you can do is wait.
