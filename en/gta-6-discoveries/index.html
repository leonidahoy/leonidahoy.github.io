Secrets and discoveries

What players are finding around Leonida: Easter eggs, references, fun facts and useful details. Only what a news outlet has checked, and no story spoilers.

15 discoveries

RecordNewNov 28

53 million copies and $4.7 billion, per an estimate

According to Forbes, analyst Alinea estimates GTA VI has sold 53 million copies and earned $4.7 billion eight days after launch. Rockstar has only confirmed $3.79 billion for day one, preorders included.

Source: Forbes
TechNewNov 28

A clip revives the theory that traffic slows fast players

According to Notebookcheck, a viral clip of a trailer swerving across a GTA VI highway has revived an old series theory: that traffic is placed to slow anyone driving very fast. Rockstar hasn't confirmed it and one clip doesn't prove it.

Source: Notebookcheck
Fun factNewNov 26

A ten-year-old song returns to the charts thanks to the game's radio

According to Queerty, Carly Rae Jepsen's "Run Away With Me" and her whole "Emotion" album are climbing the charts again because players hear the song in the game and fans are pushing it. It shows the effect of GTA VI's soundtrack on music.

Source: Queerty
ReferenceNov 25

An "It"-style clown in a Vice Beach hotel

According to Game Rant, a nod to the clown from "It" appears in a Vice Beach hotel. A writer didn't see it around 8 p.m. but did in the small hours, so it seems to depend on in-game time. It's a player find, not confirmed by Rockstar.

Source: Game Rant
Fun factNov 25

The Belville house holds messages from an NPC who knows it's all a video game

According to DualShockers, the Belville house that appeared in San Andreas in 2021 contains messages from an NPC who has discovered his world is a video game and that he is a non-playable character. It's a fourth-wall joke, unrelated to the plot.

Source: DualShockers
Easter eggNov 24

A secret portal to 2002's Vice City and Tommy Vercetti's shirt

According to Vice and Dot Esports, YouTuber Memeio found clues at electric substations that recreate a cheat from the original GTA: Vice City. Following them leads to a portal to 2002's Vice City, where you can get Tommy Vercetti's shirt. It's a community find not confirmed by Rockstar.

Sources: Vice Dot Esports
RecordNov 22

32.5 million preordered copies and nearly 8 million concurrent viewers

According to VideoCardz and Yahoo Tech, Rockstar puts copies sold in preorders alone at 32.5 million. According to Axios, cited by Yahoo Tech, nearly 8 million people watched game livestreams at once at the peak.

Sources: VideoCardz Yahoo Tech
ReferenceNov 21

A nod to GTA V's Mount Chiliad mystery

According to GamingBible, players have overheard NPCs talking about the strange events on Mount Chiliad, GTA V's unsolved puzzle for 13 years. It's a community find and Rockstar hasn't confirmed it.

Source: GamingBible
RecordNov 21

Official figure: $3.79 billion across preorders and day one

According to Variety, Take-Two says GTA VI's preorders and first-day sales added up to $3.79 billion and calls it "the biggest entertainment launch in history." It hasn't given the number of copies.

Source: Variety
SecretNov 20

A screenshot hidden in San Andreas in 2021 was from GTA VI

According to Dexerto, a screenshot that slipped into San Andreas Definitive Edition in 2021 was real: the house appears in Belville, in Leonida's Leonard County. A player from the community that maps the game located it. Rockstar hasn't said anything.

Source: Dexerto
RecordNov 20

About 19 million copies on day one, per an estimate

Sensor Tower estimates about 19.1 million copies by the morning of launch, per Men's Journal, versus GTA V's 11 million in its first 24 hours. Take-Two hasn't confirmed figures and another estimate says 17.1 million.

Sources: Men's Journal Happy Gamer
Easter eggNov 19

A literal Easter egg on top of a Vice City tower

A player found a nest with an egg at the very top of a Vice City tower. The climb takes several minutes and, if you throw the egg from the top, a surprise cutscene plays. It's Rockstar poking fun at the very idea of an Easter egg.

Sources: GameSpot IBTimes UK
ReferenceNov 19

A nod to Dexter: his apartment building is in Vice City

Players have found a building that looks like Dexter Morgan's from the Miami-set TV show. You can walk up to the door but not go in, and there's a boat on the water like the one in the show. GameSpot notes the reference is subtler than some coverage suggests.

Sources: Dexerto GameSpot
UsefulNov 19

Careful with autosave: it doesn't save all the time

The game only autosaves at certain points, and one streamer lost 11 hours of progress that way. Save manually often, especially before quitting.

Source: Vandal
Fun factNov 19

The game's made-up ketchup comes to real life

GORGE is a fictional ketchup brand seen around Leonida. Heinz has released a limited run of real bottles inspired by it.

Source: Parade
